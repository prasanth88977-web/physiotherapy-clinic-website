What Is Physiotherapy?

Helping you move, recover and live more independently.

Physiotherapy is a healthcare profession focused on movement and physical function. A physiotherapist assesses your individual needs and uses appropriate treatment strategies to help you work toward your goals.

Depending on the assessment, care may include therapeutic exercise, mobility work, strengthening, manual techniques, gait and balance training, and selected physical agents when clinically appropriate.

Our Equipment

Modern equipment for effective and safe physiotherapy.

Each piece of equipment is selected to support pain relief, mobility, strengthening, balance and rehabilitation according to your individual treatment plan.

IFT (Interferential Therapy)

Pain relief and muscle stimulation.

TENS (Pain Relief Therapy)

Helps reduce pain and muscle tension.

Ultrasound Therapy

Supports tissue healing and rehabilitation.

Shortwave Diathermy (SWD)

Deep-heat therapy for selected muscle and joint conditions.

Traction Therapy

Supports rehabilitation for selected spinal conditions.

Exercise Therapy Equipment

Strengthening, mobility and functional rehabilitation.

Parallel Bars

Useful for gait training and supported walking practice.

Balance Training Equipment

Supports balance, stability and coordination work.

Hot Pack Therapy

Moist heat for pain relief and muscle relaxation.

Cold Therapy

Helps manage swelling and inflammation.

Gym & Rehabilitation Setup

Progressive exercise programs for recovery and function.

Electrotherapy Accessories

Treatment accessories used with selected electrotherapy modalities.
